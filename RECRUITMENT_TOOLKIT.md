## Incentives Strategy

### Why Incentivize

Students are more likely to:
- Complete testing fully
- Engage daily
- Provide detailed feedback
- Become advocates

### Incentive Options

1. **Free Access** (2 weeks)
   - No cost to you (you're in early stage)
   - High perceived value ($10-20)
   - Motivates completion

2. **Certificate**
   - Free to create
   - Valuable for students (LinkedIn, resume)
   - Builds community feeling

3. **Recognition**
   - Featured on website ("Our Beta Testers")
   - Public thanks on social
   - Feels exclusive

4. **Small Gift**
   - $5-10 Amazon gift card (top engager)
   - Coffee gift card
   - Study supplies
   - Budget: $20-30 total

5. **Testimonial Feature**
   - "Featured Tester" section on site
   - Quote + photo
   - Drives engagement

### Recommended Bundle

✅ For All:
- Free 2-week access
- Certificate of completion

✅ For Top Performer:
- $10 Amazon gift card
- Featured testimonial
- Special mention in launch

## Troubleshooting Recruitment

**Problem**: Not enough sign-ups

**Solutions**:
- Post in multiple communities (Reddit, Discord, Twitter)
- Reach out to education influencers
- Ask students to share with friends
- Extend deadline
- Increase incentive (free month instead of 2 weeks)

**Problem**: Sign-ups but low engagement

**Solutions**:
- Follow-up emails (Days 3, 7)
- Reduce daily time commitment ("Even 10 min helps!")
- Add gamification (streaks, achievements)
- One-on-one outreach

**Problem**: Students dropping off mid-testing

**Solutions**:
- Identify dropoff point (Day 4? Day 10?)
- Check for bugs at that point
- Reach out personally with troubleshooting
- Offer incentive to finish ("Complete all 14 days for bonus")
